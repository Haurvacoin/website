Haurva Protocol / BNB Chain

How HAUSD works

HAUSD targets one US dollar. Haurva uses BNB as collateral and a BNB/USD price feed to calculate how much HAUSD to issue.

Explore the minting process

Minting

From BNB deposit to HAUSD

  1. Enter a BNB amount

    Choose mainnet or testnet in the minter and enter the amount of BNB you want to deposit.

  2. Review the quote

    The contract uses the BNB/USD oracle price to calculate the collateral, fee and estimated HAUSD output.

  3. Receive HAUSD

    Confirm the transaction in your wallet. Once it succeeds, you can check the issued HAUSD on BscScan.

Peg risk

What can move HAUSD away from $1?

Buyers and sellers determine the trading price. The oracle provides a BNB price for minting.

Trading liquidity
Thin liquidity can make it harder to trade HAUSD near $1.
Collateral value
A drop in BNB’s price can reduce the dollar value of the reserve.
Protocol failures
Oracle outages and smart-contract bugs can disrupt protocol operations.
