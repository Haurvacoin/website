How HAUSD works

HAUSD is a stablecoin on BNB Chain that targets one US dollar. The minting contract accepts BNB and uses a BNB/USD price feed to calculate how much HAUSD to issue.

From BNB deposit to HAUSD

  1. Choose a network and enter a BNB amount in the minter.
  2. The contract quote shows the collateral portion, fee and estimated HAUSD output. Review these amounts before confirming.
  3. When the transaction succeeds, HAUSD is issued to the wallet. You can verify the transaction in a block explorer.

What can move HAUSD away from $1?

The oracle prices BNB for minting. HAUSD trades at prices set by buyers and sellers. Thin liquidity can make it hard to trade near $1, and a fall in BNB’s price can reduce the dollar value of the collateral. Oracle and smart-contract failures can also disrupt protocol operations.
