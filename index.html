Haurva Protocol

Disrupting Stable Coins

Haurva Protocol is a stable coin mechanism relying on native coins of the blockchain (BNB for now) instead of fiat money and it keeps its own price exactly at the pre-determined price using smart contracts and algorithms.

01 / Protocol

Algorithmic stable coin system.

Our algorithmic smart contracts check the price of the native coin using oracles and then align the price exactly at one dollars or any other currency we're trying to tokenize. It is different from Ethena and it's based on your own crypto reserves.

Currently, we're only focusing on US Dollars as our main goal. The protocol is different from Tether or Circle and doesn't rely on the fiat money or bank account. Instead, we offer a central EVM compatible vault.

02 / Philosophy

No fiat money, but value saved!

Most stablecoins such as USDT, USDC or XAUt are relying on a fiat currency. You have to pay them fiat money and get crypto version. It is hard, has the risk of your assets being frozen and also redeeming can be hard. Our protocol solved these problems.

Our main philosophy is basically Your asset shall be yours under every circumstances so we do not have a freeze function. Also, we offer quick redemption, as quick as swapping your currency on our very own swapper.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Haurva Protocol is a stablecoin protocol which aligns its price using the price of the native token of the blockchain. Our protocol is basically our very own way of making an Algorithmic Stablecoin.
Haurva Protocol connects to the oracle (for now BNB/USD) and gets the price of the native coin, then aligns its own price to exactly one US Dollar and also takes %0.05 minting fee per coin. Also, when you redeem it, it does it in reverse and gives you back the token you spent.
Every place you can use crypto currencies and stablecoins, you can use Haurva Protocol as well.
